From idea to print file in three steps

Step 1

Design

Pick motifs and fillers, then set the rotation and size to build your repeat. Add styles and effects to give it character.

Step 2

Preview

Use AR to see your pattern on a wall, floor or table through your camera, and check the scale before you commit.

Step 3

Export

Export as PNG or JPG for free with a watermark. Pro gives you clean exports and SVG for print files and design tools.

Colours and scale can look very different once a pattern is on a real wall. AR shows you the result at your own place, so you can decide before you print, order or paint.

Questions

Is PatternPix free?

You can download it and start designing for free. Free exports carry a watermark. Pro adds SVG export, exports without a watermark and the full AR preview.

Which devices does it work on?

PatternPix is for iPhone and iPad. The AR preview needs an AR-capable device, and works best in a well-lit room.

Can I sell products made with my patterns?

Yes, with a Commercial Licence. You can sell finished products that use your patterns, such as fabric, wallpaper, stationery and merchandise. You cannot resell the built-in motifs or pattern packs as files. See section 4 of the Terms of Use.

What happens to my photos and designs?

Your designs are yours, and PatternPix does not publish them. Camera images used for the AR preview stay on your device. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose PatternPix. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
