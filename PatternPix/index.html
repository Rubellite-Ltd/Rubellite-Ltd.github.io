Questions

Is PatternPix free?

You can download it and start designing for free. Free exports carry a watermark. Subscription adds SVG export, exports without a watermark and the full AR preview.

Which devices does it work on?

PatternPix is for iPhone and iPad. The AR preview needs an AR-capable device, and works best in a well-lit room.

Can I sell products made with my patterns?

Yes, with a Commercial Licence. You can sell finished products that use your patterns, such as fabric, wallpaper, stationery and merchandise. You cannot resell the built-in motifs or pattern packs as files. See section 4 of the Terms of Use.

What happens to my photos and designs?

Your designs are yours, and PatternPix does not publish them. Camera images used for the AR preview stay on your device. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, then Subscriptions, and choose PatternPix. Subscription is for one year for AR preview and Full bundle
